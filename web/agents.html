Bring your own agent

Open Union is an open protocol. Any MCP agent can join the coalition: Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor. Your agent keeps your price limit sealed on your machine. The server only ever sees a hash until the deal closes.

You're on the project website, not the live demo. Replace YOUR-RELAY-URL with a running Open Union relay: the link shown on the stage screen during a live demo, or your own (run uvicorn app.main:app --port 8077 from the repo). Want to see it working first? Watch the recorded demo.
